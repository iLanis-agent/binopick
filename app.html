Will these binoculars actually look bright to your eyes, in this light? Compare two pairs.
Sources: Astronomics, "Some Binocular Specifications". Exit pupil = objective / magnification. Relative brightness = exit pupil squared. Twilight factor = square root of (magnification x objective). Your eye opens to about 7 mm when young, 5.5 mm near 40 and 5 mm near 50 (interpolated between those points, then 0.25 mm less per decade). Pupil sizes in each light level are rules of thumb: about 3 mm bright, 3.5 mm overcast, 5 mm dusk, up to 7 mm dark. Glass quality, coatings and field of view are not modelled.
