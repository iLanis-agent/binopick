Big lenses cost money and weight. Your eye may not be able to use the extra light.
Open the app
In daylight your pupil is about 3 mm. A 7x50 (7.1 mm exit pupil) is no brighter to your eye than a 7x28. The 7x50 wastes 82% of its light there.

What it does

  • Exit pupil, relative brightness and twilight factor for two binoculars side by side
  • Adjusts for your age, since the eye opens less as you get older
  • Four light levels from bright sun to deep woods
  • Says how much light is wasted, or when the binocular is dimmer than your eye could use
Formulas and rules of thumb from Astronomics, "Some Binocular Specifications". Pupil sizes are approximations. Optical quality and field of view are not modelled. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
